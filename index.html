How to play

  1. Fill every row, column, and 3 × 3 box with 1–9, without repeating a number.
  2. Select a square, then tap a number. You can also use arrow keys and number keys.
  3. Use Erase to change your entry, or Get a hint when you need a hand.
  4. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard. Pause whenever you like—there is no mistake limit.

QUICK GUIDE

The essentials

  1. Fill each empty square with a number from 1 to 9.
  2. Each row, column, and bold 3 × 3 box must contain every number once.
  3. Select a square, then tap a number below the board or use your keyboard.
  4. Stuck? Get a hint fills the selected square. Use Erase to clear your entry.
More about the controls

Bold numbers are starting clues and cannot be changed. With Show mistakes enabled, wrong entries have an exclamation mark and a visible mistake counter. Settings lets you adjust sounds, matching-number highlights, and mistake feedback. There is no mistake limit. Hints are unlimited and counted separately. If a clue or a correct square is selected, a hint fills another unfinished square. Use arrow keys to move, 1–9 to enter, and Delete or Backspace to erase. Choose a difficulty to start a new puzzle.

STRATEGY

Start with the gaps

Look for a row, column, or box with just a few missing numbers. Small wins add up.
